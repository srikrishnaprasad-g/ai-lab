# Product AI Portfolio Roadmap

**Project:** Product AI Portfolio  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** Sri Krishna Prasad

---

# 1. Vision

Build a world-class portfolio website that positions Sri Krishna Prasad as a Product Leader who combines strategic product thinking, technical depth, and practical AI expertise.

The portfolio should demonstrate not only past achievements but also current capabilities through real projects, structured thinking, and exceptional execution.

---

# 2. Product Goals

The website should:

- Differentiate from traditional PM portfolios
- Increase recruiter and hiring manager engagement
- Showcase measurable business impact
- Demonstrate AI-native product management capabilities
- Highlight technical product leadership
- Serve as a long-term professional brand

---

# 3. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

- The portfolio is publicly accessible.
- It clearly communicates professional value within the first 30 seconds.
- Hiring managers can quickly understand key achievements.
- Case studies demonstrate product thinking rather than resume content.
- The website achieves Lighthouse scores above 95 across all categories.
- The website is fully responsive and accessible.
- The website becomes the primary professional portfolio.

# 6. Sprint Execution Process

Every sprint follows the Engineering Playbook.

1. Sprint Planning
2. Documentation Review
3. Sprint Prompt Creation
4. Gemini Implementation
5. Quality Gate 1 – Local Validation
6. Architecture Review (ChatGPT)
7. Gemini Corrections
8. Quality Gate 2 – Build & Tests
9. Git Commit
10. Push to GitHub
11. Quality Gate 3 – Repository Review
12. Sprint Closure

---

# 7. Risks

Potential risks include:

- Scope creep
- Overengineering
- Performance regressions
- Documentation drift
- Inconsistent design language
- Delays due to excessive polish

Mitigation:

- Strict sprint scope
- Documentation-first development
- Incremental releases
- Regular architecture reviews
