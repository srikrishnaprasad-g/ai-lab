# Website Strategy

**Project:** Product AI Portfolio  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** Sri Krishna Prasad

---

# 1. Vision

Build a world-class digital portfolio that positions Sri Krishna Prasad as a Product Leader who combines business strategy, technical depth, and practical AI expertise.

The website should communicate not only past achievements but also demonstrate current capabilities through real projects, structured thinking, and high-quality execution.

---

# 2. Primary Goals

The website should:

- Differentiate from traditional PM portfolios.
- Showcase product thinking rather than resume content.
- Demonstrate measurable business impact.
- Highlight AI-native product management capabilities.
- Increase interview conversion.
- Establish personal brand credibility.
- Serve as a long-term professional website.

---

# 3. Target Audience

## Primary

- Hiring Managers
- Directors of Product
- VP Product
- Chief Product Officers
- Founders

## Secondary

- Recruiters
- Startup founders
- Product peers
- Engineering leaders
- Design leaders

---

# 4. Positioning Statement

> Product Leader with 13+ years of experience building enterprise SaaS platforms and AI-enabled products that deliver measurable customer and business outcomes.

---

# 5. Brand Attributes

The website should feel:

- Professional
- Modern
- Intelligent
- Trustworthy
- Minimal
- Technical
- Strategic

Avoid:

- Flashy animations
- Excessive gradients
- Template-style layouts
- Buzzword-heavy messaging

---

# 6. Key Differentiators

The website should emphasize:

- Product Leadership
- Platform Product Management
- AI Product Strategy
- Technical Product Management
- Data-driven decision making
- Cross-functional leadership
- AI experimentation through AI-Lab

---

# 7. Information Architecture

## Home

Overview and introduction.

## About

Professional story.

## Experience

Career journey.

## Case Studies

Deep dives into selected products.

## AI Lab

Experiments and AI projects.

## Writing

Articles, frameworks, and documentation.

## Resume

Downloadable resume.

## Contact

Professional contact information.

---

# 8. Content Strategy

Every page should answer one question.

| Page | Primary Question |
|-------|------------------|
| Home | Who is this person? |
| About | Why should I trust them? |
| Experience | What have they accomplished? |
| Case Studies | How do they think? |
| AI Lab | What are they building now? |
| Writing | How do they communicate? |
| Contact | How can I reach them? |

---

# 9. Case Study Framework

Each case study follows a consistent structure.

1. Problem
2. Context
3. Constraints
4. Discovery
5. Product Strategy
6. Execution
7. Outcomes
8. Metrics
9. Learnings

---

# 10. User Experience Principles

The website should be:

- Easy to scan
- Mobile responsive
- Fast
- Accessible
- Keyboard friendly
- Minimal navigation depth

Users should reach any major section within two clicks.

---

# 11. Visual Design Principles

Design should prioritize readability over decoration.

Typography should lead the experience.

Whitespace should be intentional.

Animations should support usability rather than distract.

---

# 12. Performance Goals

- Lighthouse Performance > 95
- Accessibility > 95
- Best Practices > 95
- SEO > 95

---

# 13. Accessibility

Follow WCAG 2.2 AA guidelines.

Ensure:

- Keyboard navigation
- Sufficient contrast
- Semantic HTML
- Screen reader compatibility
- Responsive typography

---

# 14. SEO Strategy

Primary keywords:

- Product Manager
- Principal Product Manager
- AI Product Manager
- Technical Product Manager
- Product Leadership
- Product Strategy

Each page should have:

- Unique title
- Meta description
- Open Graph metadata
- Structured headings

---

# 15. Technology Principles

The solution should prioritize:

- Maintainability
- Performance
- Scalability
- Component reusability
- Clean architecture

---

# 16. Success Metrics

The website is successful when it:

- Clearly communicates professional value within 30 seconds.
- Encourages visitors to explore case studies.
- Increases recruiter engagement.
- Supports interview conversations.
- Serves as a living portfolio for future work.

---

# 17. Future Enhancements

Potential future additions include:

- Blog
- Speaking engagements
- Product frameworks
- AI demos
- Interactive product simulations
- Newsletter
- Analytics dashboard

---

# 18. Non-Goals

The website will not attempt to:

- Function as a social network.
- Include unnecessary visual effects.
- Showcase every project ever completed.
- Duplicate the content of LinkedIn.
- Prioritize aesthetics over clarity.

---

# 19. Guiding Principle

Every design decision should answer one question:

> "Does this help a hiring manager better understand Sri Krishna Prasad's ability to build great products?"
