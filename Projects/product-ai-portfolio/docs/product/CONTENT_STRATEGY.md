# Content Strategy

## Purpose

The Content Strategy defines the messaging, storytelling approach, information hierarchy, and content guidelines for the Product AI Portfolio.

The objective is to ensure every page communicates a consistent professional narrative while supporting the portfolio's goals of demonstrating product leadership, technical depth, and AI expertise.

---

# Content Objectives

The portfolio should:

- Establish credibility quickly.
- Showcase measurable business impact.
- Demonstrate product thinking.
- Highlight AI-assisted development practices.
- Encourage recruiters, hiring managers, and collaborators to make contact.

---

# Target Audience

## Primary

- Hiring Managers
- Product Leaders
- Founders
- CTOs
- VP Product

---

## Secondary

- Recruiters
- Engineering Managers
- Product Managers
- AI Engineers
- Startup Teams

---

## Tertiary

- Students
- Product Community
- AI Enthusiasts

---

# Core Narrative

The visitor journey should naturally answer four questions:

1. **Who are you?**
2. **Why should I trust you?**
3. **What have you built?**
4. **How can I work with you?**

This aligns with the visitor journey defined during solution architecture:

**Identity → Credibility → Evidence → Action**

---

# Content Pillars

## Professional Identity

Introduce:

- Product Management experience
- Industries served
- Leadership philosophy
- Areas of expertise

---

## Product Portfolio

Showcase:

- Product case studies
- Business outcomes
- Product strategy
- Execution approach
- Lessons learned

Focus on outcomes rather than responsibilities.

---

## AI Lab

Demonstrate:

- AI experiments
- Multi-agent workflows
- Automation projects
- Engineering methodology
- Learning journey

---

## Career Journey

Present a concise professional timeline highlighting:

- Key companies
- Major responsibilities
- Career progression
- Significant achievements

---

## Resume

Provide an up-to-date downloadable resume with clear calls to action.

---

## Contact

Offer multiple ways to connect and encourage meaningful conversations.

---

# Writing Guidelines

Every page should be:

- Clear
- Concise
- Professional
- Authentic
- Outcome-focused

Avoid unnecessary jargon and lengthy paragraphs.

---

# Tone of Voice

The portfolio should sound:

- Confident
- Knowledgeable
- Practical
- Collaborative
- Approachable

Avoid exaggerated claims or buzzword-heavy language.

---

# Content Principles

- Lead with customer and business outcomes.
- Support claims with evidence where possible.
- Use metrics to demonstrate impact.
- Prefer active voice.
- Keep messaging consistent across all pages.

---

# SEO Considerations

Content should naturally incorporate relevant topics such as:

- Product Management
- AI Product Management
- Product Strategy
- Product Leadership
- AI Engineering
- SaaS
- Enterprise Software
- Healthcare Technology
- Portfolio
- Case Studies

---

# Calls to Action

Each major page should include a clear next step, such as:

- View Product Portfolio
- Explore AI Lab
- Download Resume
- Contact Me

---

# Content Governance

Content should be reviewed whenever:

- A significant project is completed.
- A new role or achievement is added.
- The resume is updated.
- Messaging or positioning changes.
