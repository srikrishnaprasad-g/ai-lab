# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Project Name

**product-ai-portfolio**

---

# 1. Vision

Build a world-class Product & AI portfolio that showcases Sri Krishna Prasad's product leadership, technical depth, AI engineering work, and thought process to recruiters, hiring managers, founders, peers, and the broader product community.

The website will serve as the single source of truth for the user's professional identity while evolving alongside future work within AI-Lab.

---

# 2. Mission

Create a professional platform that demonstrates evidence of product leadership through real outcomes, documented case studies, AI engineering projects, and structured thinking rather than functioning as a traditional résumé website.

---

# 3. Success Metrics

## Primary

* Visitors understand the professional profile within 2–3 minutes.
* Hiring managers can quickly evaluate product leadership and technical capabilities.
* Every major project is represented by a well-documented case study.
* The website ranks highly for searches related to the user's name.

## Secondary

* Increased interview opportunities.
* Increased recruiter outreach.
* Speaking and mentoring invitations.
* Growth in professional network.
* Organic traffic from LinkedIn, GitHub, and search engines.

---

# 4. Target Audience

## Primary

* Hiring Managers
* Product Leaders
* Recruiters

## Secondary

* Startup Founders
* Product Managers
* AI Builders
* Engineering Leaders

---

# 5. Product Positioning

This is **not** intended to be:

* A résumé website
* A personal blog
* A digital business card
* A static portfolio

Instead, it is:

> A Product Leadership & AI Innovation platform demonstrating how strategic product thinking and practical AI engineering combine to solve meaningful customer problems.

---

# 6. Core Value Proposition

Visitors should leave with one clear impression:

> Sri Krishna Prasad combines enterprise product leadership with hands-on AI engineering and builds systems that create measurable business impact.

---

---

# 8. Product Principles

1. Every page should answer: **Why should someone hire or collaborate with me?**

2. Every important claim should be supported by evidence.

3. Outcomes take precedence over responsibilities.

4. The experience should remain clean, focused, and distraction-free.

5. AI should be demonstrated through meaningful work rather than visual gimmicks.

---

# 9. MVP Scope

Version 1 will include:

* Home
* About
* Career Journey
* Product Portfolio
* AI Lab
* Resume
* Contact

---

# 10. Out of Scope (Version 1)

The following are intentionally excluded:

* User authentication
* Comments
* Newsletter
* CMS
* Search
* AI chatbot
* Complex animations
* Advanced personalization

These will be evaluated after the initial launch.

---

# 11. Technical Goals

The platform should be:

* Fast
* Responsive
* Accessible
* SEO-friendly
* Easy to maintain
* Easy to extend
* Based on open standards
* Free to host initially

---

# 12. Long-Term Vision

The portfolio should evolve into the public face of AI-Lab.

Future phases may include:

* Product Case Studies
* Product Thinking Library
* Speaking
* Writing
* Open Source Contributions
* AI Experiments
* Interactive Demonstrations
* Learning Journal
* Resource Library

---

# Definition of Success

The website should function as more than a portfolio. It should become an evolving professional platform that demonstrates product leadership, AI engineering capability, and continuous learning through authentic, evidence-backed work.
