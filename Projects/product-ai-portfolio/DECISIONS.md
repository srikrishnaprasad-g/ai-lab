## 2026-07-26 — Documentation-First Development

### Decision

All implementation work will follow the Documentation First methodology.

### Rationale

Reduce ambiguity, improve maintainability, and ensure AI-assisted development remains aligned with product goals.

### Impact

Every implementation sprint must begin by reviewing the approved documentation and end by updating operational documents.

---

## 2026-07-26 — AI-Assisted Development Workflow

### Decision

Adopt a role-based AI workflow.

### Responsibilities

**ChatGPT**

- Product Management
- Architecture
- Engineering Review
- Documentation
- Sprint Planning

**Gemini CLI**

- Implementation
- Refactoring
- Bug Fixes
- Code Generation

### Impact

Clear separation of responsibilities improves consistency and governance.
